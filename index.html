How to read this. The risk score is a rough heuristic built from public pool stats (liquidity, age, volume, buy/sell balance, valuation). It cannot see honeypot code, hidden mint functions or rug pulls, so a low score does not mean safe, and a high score does not prove a scam. "Boosted" tokens are paid promotions. "New pools" and "Trending" use GeckoTerminal to discover pools and DexScreener for the details, so some pools may be missing (the line under the controls shows how many). Brand-new pairs are where most scams live. Liquidity alerts can also fire when a token's price simply falls, since pool value is measured in dollars. Profit race

Replays past prices with a 0.1% fee per trade and no slippage, so real results would be worse. A handful of trades proves nothing, and past performance does not predict future results. A research toy, not a signal, not financial advice.
This tab reads a trade-history file you give it, inside your browser only. Nothing is uploaded, and it has no connection to Alpaca, Railway or any account.
What file does it need? (and what to ask your bot thread for)

One row per closed trade. It looks for these columns, in any order, and only needs one way to work out the result:

• result: pnl_pct (percent, so 2.5 means 2.5%) or pnl_usd or entry_price + exit_price
• helpful extras: symbol, closed_at (date-time), side, qty

Paste this into your bot thread:

Please give me an export of all CLOSED paper trades as a CSV with these columns: symbol, closed_at (ISO date-time), side, entry_price, exit_price, qty, pnl_usd, pnl_pct (as a percent, so 2.5 means 2.5%). Also tell me each strategy's backtest win rate, average return per trade, and max drawdown, so I can compare them.
