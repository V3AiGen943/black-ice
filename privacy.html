Not financial advice

Black Ice is a research and education tool. Risk scores are simple rules applied to public pool data. They cannot see contract intent, hidden code, or what insiders plan to do. A LOW score does not mean safe. Nothing here is a recommendation to buy or sell. Past performance does not predict future results. You can lose everything you put into any token.

What stays on your device

Black Ice has no accounts, no wallet connection and no trading. Your watchlist, settings and any keys you paste are stored only in your own browser (localStorage). We do not run a server and do not receive them.

What your browser requests

To show data, your browser contacts public services directly: DexScreener, GeckoTerminal, Binance and Coinbase public market data, and (only if you click Contract check) GoPlus for EVM tokens or a Solana RPC node. Those services see your IP address under their own policies. Contract check data is provided by GoPlus.

Sample data

If a live feed is unreachable, the app may show clearly labelled sample data. Replays can use synthetic prices when market data is unavailable and say so on screen.
