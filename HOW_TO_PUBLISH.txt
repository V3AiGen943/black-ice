BLACK ICE: PUT IT ON GITHUB PAGES (free, about 10 minutes)

Files in this folder (upload all of them EXCEPT this one, if you like):
  index.html, manifest.webmanifest, sw.js, icon-192.png, icon-512.png, privacy.html

STEP 1  Make the repository
  - Go to github.com, click "+" (top right), "New repository".
  - Name: black-ice   (lowercase, no spaces)
  - Choose Public. (Free GitHub Pages needs Public. The page can't be hidden,
    but nobody finds it unless you send the link.)
  - Tick "Add a README file". Click "Create repository".

STEP 2  Upload the files
  - In the new repo click "Add file" > "Upload files".
  - Drag in ALL the files from this folder (not the folder itself).
  - Click "Commit changes".

STEP 3  Turn on Pages
  - Repo "Settings" > "Pages" (left menu).
  - Under "Build and deployment" set Source: "Deploy from a branch".
  - Branch: main, folder: / (root). Click Save.
  - Wait 1 to 2 minutes, refresh. A box shows your address:
      https://YOUR-USERNAME.github.io/black-ice/

STEP 4  Check it
  - Open the address. Green LIVE dot means feeds work.
  - Phone or desktop Chrome/Edge: use "Install app" (address bar icon or menu).
  - iPhone Safari: Share > Add to Home Screen.

STEP 5  Send the link to a few friends
  - Ask them: what confused you, what broke, which tab did you use?

NOTES
  - The https address is secured automatically (GitHub provides it).
  - After you update a file, hard-refresh (Ctrl+F5) once to get the new version.
  - Name lives in index.html (<title>) and manifest.webmanifest, plus the header text.
